01 / 04 · Brand Identity

Lumen — Coffee Rebrand

A specialty roaster with exceptional beans and zero shelf presence. We rebuilt the brand to finally match the quality in the bag.

RoleBrand Identity & Art Direction
Year2024
ServicesStrategy · Identity · Packaging
TypeRebrand
OverviewThe brief

Lumen had a loyal café following but was invisible on retail shelves. The product was genuinely premium; the branding said "corner coffee shop." The goal was to give Lumen the presence to compete in stores and the confidence to expand beyond its four walls.

01Challenge

Next to slicker competitors, Lumen disappeared. The old logo and packaging undersold the quality inside, capping growth and making retail buyers hesitant. The brand needed to look as considered as it tasted — without losing the warmth regulars loved.

02Approach

We started with positioning — premium, warm, quietly confident — then built a full identity system around it: a custom wordmark, a bold palette anchored in deep black and molten orange, and a flexible packaging layout engineered to read from across the aisle.

Every touchpoint, from the bag to the social grid, was designed to feel intentional and unmistakably Lumen.

03Outcome

The rebrand gave Lumen shelf presence and the confidence to scale. Within four months, retail sell-through climbed and two regional chains signed on as stockists.
