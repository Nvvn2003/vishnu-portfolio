04 / 04 · Rebrand

Pulse — Fintech Rebrand

A finance app that felt cold to the exact users it wanted to win. We warmed it up — without losing the credibility.

RoleRebrand & Marketing
Year2022
ServicesRebrand · UI Direction · Rollout
TypeRebrand
OverviewThe brief

Pulse wanted a younger, mobile-first audience but its brand felt institutional and stiff. The challenge was to feel approachable and modern while keeping the trust a finance product depends on.

01Challenge

The old identity read as cold and corporate — a turn-off for the people Pulse was chasing. Trust and approachability were pulling in opposite directions, and the app store presence wasn't converting.

02Approach

We repositioned Pulse around clarity and momentum: a confident new identity, a friendlier UI direction, and a coordinated App Store and social rollout that made the relaunch feel like an event rather than an update.

Warmer, sharper, and still unmistakably a finance brand you can trust.

03Outcome

The new Pulse connected with its target audience. Ratings climbed post-relaunch and early retention jumped — the brand finally felt like it belonged on a younger user's home screen.
